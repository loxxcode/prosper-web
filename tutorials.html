Tutorials

Follow practical steps to improve your digital habits, protect your devices, and get more from the tools you use every day.

Secure Your Accounts

  • Use a unique password for every important account.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Review account recovery details and active sessions.

Keep Your Computer Healthy

  • Install operating system and application updates.
  • Back up important files to a separate safe location.
  • Remove software you no longer use and scan downloads.

Build a Better Website Brief

  • Describe your audience and the goals of the website.
  • Gather your brand assets, content, and contact details.
  • List the features visitors need on desktop and mobile.
